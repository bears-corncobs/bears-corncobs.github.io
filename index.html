hermes-mail-readonly

hermes-mail-readonly is a private, single-user tool. It lets its owner's own personal AI assistant (running on the owner's own computer) read the owner's own Gmail mailbox in read-only mode, so that it can send the owner a short notification when a new message needs attention (for example, a notice from a school) and a daily summary of other messages.

What the app does

  • Requests one Google permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (view email messages and settings).
  • Checks for new messages periodically and classifies them as urgent, routine, or promotional.
  • Sends the owner a short notification (sender and subject) through a private messaging channel.
  • Never sends, deletes, modifies, or labels any email. It has no permission to do so.

Who can use it

This app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not a commercial product and has no other users.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
